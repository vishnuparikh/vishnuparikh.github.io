GTA Clips Studio

A personal tool that turns GTA 6 gameplay into short vertical clips for the YouTube channel @vishnuparikh.

What it does

  • Finds GTA 6 videos on YouTube whose creators published them under the Creative Commons Attribution licence ("reuse allowed").
  • Cuts the most exciting moments into short vertical clips with captions, on the owner's own computer.
  • Uploads those clips to the owner's YouTube channel, with a credit and a link to the original video in every description, as the licence requires.

Who uses it

GTA Clips Studio is used only by its owner, to manage their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Google and YouTube data

The app signs in with Google only to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's name. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is kept.

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
