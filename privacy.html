Privacy Policy

GTA Clips Studio · Last updated 21 November 2026

GTA Clips Studio ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner to make short clips and upload them to their own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses through Google and YouTube, and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects their YouTube account, the app asks Google for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish clips to the owner's channel.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and ID, so the app can show which channel is connected.

The app does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, playlists, subscribers or any other account data, and it does not access any Google service other than YouTube.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in tokens Google returns, and the channel's name and ID, are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the app's local data folder. They are used only to upload clips the owner's settings choose.
  • No data is sent to the app's developer or to any third party, and none is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Videos the app processes are public videos licensed under Creative Commons Attribution. They are downloaded and cut on the owner's computer, and each uploaded clip credits and links to the original.

Keeping or deleting the data

The data stays on the owner's computer until the owner disconnects the account in the app or deletes the app's data folder. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions or, for YouTube, from Google security settings.

Google and YouTube policies

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
